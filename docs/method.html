About this guide

Practical knowledge from the longboarding community.

Ultra Skate Fieldnotes brings together experience shared over the years by longboarders preparing for, riding and returning from Ultraskates. These guides help you plan your training, equipment, food and the practical details of a long day on the board.

People approach the same challenge in different ways. Where experiences differ, the guides explain the circumstances and trade-offs so you can make an informed choice. References lead to the original contributions and further reading.
